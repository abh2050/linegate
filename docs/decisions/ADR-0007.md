## Finding

No evaluated batch exceeded the reference by even one noise SD; four of six
fell more than 1.5 SD below it. Together with the Gate 1 ablation (ADR-0003),
where out-of-range counts, route target encoding, and all raw numeric columns
also failed to help, the evidence is that `baseline_v2` already captures the
honest, time-stable signal this split offers to a LightGBM of this shape.
Extra columns mostly let the model fit the older, higher-failure train period.
